# Lobster artwork

`lobster-hero.webp`, `lobster-claw.webp`, and `lobster-avatar.webp` are generated illustrative assets for claw-chat, produced on September 14, 2026 with OpenAI's image generation service (gpt-image-2), then resized/compressed locally. They are not documentary wildlife photographs. No real person's photo or third-party reference image was supplied.

Art direction: dramatic scarlet/oxblood lobster, glossy shell and claws, dark burgundy background, editorial studio lighting. Wide negative space for welcome-screen typography; no text baked into the images. The avatar is a cropped derivative of the hero.
