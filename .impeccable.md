## Design Context

### Users
OpenClaw community members managing substantial local agent workflows: conversations, parallel tasks, checkpoints, and curated project context. Desktop-first, single-operator V1. They need to understand and direct work without reading every transcript.

### Brand Personality
Lobstery, confident, playful. Owner direction, September 14, 2026: red branding, glamorous lobster photography, and lobsters that periodically offer encouragement. Keep operational facts and consequential controls plain and precise.

### Aesthetic Direction
Lobster/scarlet red brand accents, warm shell-white light surfaces, dark ink/oxblood dark surfaces. Dramatic editorial lobster photography, glossy shells, oversized claws, rich shadows. Both light and dark modes. Photographs primarily on welcome/empty screens and optional small mascot moments, not behind transcripts. No neon, generic dashboard card grids, decorative metrics, or continuously animated graphs. Brand red must not be the sole indication of failure: status labels/icons and accessible contrast remain mandatory.

### Design Principles
1. Chat, context provenance, and decision evidence remain readable; photography supports rather than obscures the work.
2. Distinguish a lobster's encouragement from verified task status and human approval.
3. Keep the active workspace compact; expose details on demand.
4. Delight is local, optional, quiet, dismissible, and respectful of reduced motion. No model calls or background notifications just to generate praise.
5. Serious warnings, approval reviews, and failures take priority over mascot messages.

### Hands-on onboarding
The approved MVP offers an optional **Try a guided example** beside **Explore on my own**, replayable as **Learn by doing** from the demo workspace. Use one compact, responsive inline instruction next to the existing controls. No overlay, copied controls, auto-approval, or automatic stage launch. Practice is explicitly synthetic and makes no model calls. Suppress optional mascot chatter during the guide; operational errors and human review controls remain visible. Only dismissal/completion preferences persist locally, without source text. Restart begins an unbound selection and preserves existing projects.
